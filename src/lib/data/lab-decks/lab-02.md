@prose
## Notebook

When production is infinitely expansive, then how do ideas and the experience around formulating ideas take precedence over the act of producing?

Writing and drawing in a notebook and then transferring that to the digital surface in practical terms is inefficient, but in practice may be more powerful.

The technology ironically opens up this possibility because of its flexibility and elevated sensibilities.
